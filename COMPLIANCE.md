# CCSDSPack v1.2 compliance statement

## Release claim

CCSDSPack v1.2 implements a **CCSDS 133.0-B-2, Issue 2, including Editorial Change 2, Space Packet PDU profile**.

The claim applies to the supported packet construction, serialization, bounded parsing, validation, segmentation, sequence-count, and single-stream management behaviour described in the repository documentation.

## Covered scope

The v1.2 profile covers:

- the fixed six-octet CCSDS Space Packet Primary Header;
- Packet Data Field construction and exact Packet Data Length encoding;
- Packet Version Number `000` enforcement at the `Packet` boundary;
- telemetry and telecommand Packet Types;
- the complete 11-bit APID range and Idle Packet structural checks;
- CCSDS Sequence Flags and modulo-16384 Packet Sequence Count handling;
- exact one-packet parsing with consumed-byte reporting;
- concatenated packet-stream processing;
- deterministic, non-mutating packet inspection;
- independently generated byte vectors and negative tests.

## Claim boundary

CCSDSPack v1.2 does **not** claim implementation of:

- the complete abstract CCSDS Packet Service;
- the complete abstract CCSDS Octet String Service;
- every sending, receiving, transfer, and management procedure of a complete protocol entity;
- every managed parameter defined by CCSDS 133.0-B-2;
- a completed Protocol Implementation Conformance Statement (PICS);
- transfer frames, virtual channels, CFDP, COP-1, or transport bindings;
- an official ECSS Packet Utilisation Standard implementation.

The bundled `PusA`, `PusB`, and `PusC` classes are retained as legacy project-specific secondary-header formats. Their names are not an ECSS PUS compliance claim.

## CCSDSPack mission-profile features

The optional CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE trailer is a **CCSDSPack mission-profile convention** stored in the final two octets of the Packet Data Field. CCSDS 133.0-B-2 does not define it as a separate Space Packet field.

Supported packet-error-control modes are:

- `PacketErrorControlMode::CRC16`, the source-compatible v1 default;
- `PacketErrorControlMode::None`.

A configurable synchronization pattern may be used around packet streams by `CCSDS::Manager`. It is external framing and is not part of a CCSDS Space Packet.

## Compatibility

The v1 public source API remains available. Corrected wire behaviour in v1.2 can change packet bytes or reject packets accepted by earlier releases. Pre-v1.2 stored or transmitted packets should be regenerated or migrated explicitly.
