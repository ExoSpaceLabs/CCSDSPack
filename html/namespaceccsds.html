enum ccsds::EHeaderStatus : std::uint8_t

Indicates whether a Header contains a normal, idle, or invalid APID state.

INVALID is a fail-safe state entered when checked field assignment fails. An invalid header serializes to an empty byte vector and reports zero as its packed value. A later successful APID/header assignment restores NORMAL or IDLE.

Enumerator
NORMAL 

APID is in the normal range 0..2046 and all fields are valid.

IDLE 

APID equals the reserved idle value 2047.

INVALID 

At least one checked assignment failed; serialization is suppressed.

◆ ESequenceFlag

enum ccsds::ESequenceFlag : std::uint8_t

Values of the two-bit CCSDS Space Packet sequence-flags field.

The flags describe whether a packet is complete or one segment of a larger application-data sequence. They belong to the Space Packet primary header, not to a transfer-frame header.

Enumerator
CONTINUING_SEGMENT 

Binary 00: an intermediate segment.

FIRST_SEGMENT 

Binary 01: first segment of a segmented sequence.

LAST_SEGMENT 

Binary 10: final segment of a segmented sequence.

UNSEGMENTED 

Binary 11: a complete, unsegmented packet.
