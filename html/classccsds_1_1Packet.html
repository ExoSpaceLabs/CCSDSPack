Detailed Description

Owns, serializes, and parses one CCSDS Space Packet PDU.

Packet-level policy such as CRC16 remains independent of secondary-header type. Directional secondary headers may report PacketDirection through SecondaryHeaderAbstract::getDirection(); installing one synchronizes the CCSDS primary-header Packet Type. PUS revision/direction and PUS layout tailoring are owned by the concrete PUS secondary-header object rather than by Packet.
