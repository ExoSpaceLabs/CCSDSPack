Parses and validates one bounded packet without allocating or copying.

Extra bytes after the first declared packet are permitted and reported only through size - view->consumed, making this suitable for packet streams.

When error_control is CRC16, crc_config may be NULL to select the default CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE parameters.
